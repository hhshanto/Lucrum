| 2026-10-28 | **G-8 decided under the standing escalation rule: the tiebreak for "most recent import attempt" is a monotonic insertion sequence, NOT `id`.** `spec-oracle` confirms the specification is *uniformly silent* -- `period-v1` (`walmart-user-stories.md:137-138`) resolves UTC storage and DST date ambiguity but says nothing about ordering two rows sharing an instant; US-005 AC-03 (`:791`) says "the last successful update and failure are visible" without defining "last" under a tie; the `import_batches` schema entry (`database-schema.md:967-983`) defines no secondary sort key. Not escalated: it is not profit, not closed-month/historical financials, and not a contradiction (no two documents disagree -- both are silent). **The tempting fix is wrong and must be refused by name.** `import_batches.id` is `uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid()` (`004_import_tracking.sql:28`), so `order by started_at desc, id desc` is deterministic but **arbitrary** -- under a tie it picks by random UUID, reporting the *older* attempt as the most recent roughly half the time.
